Sandbox: drive the rules

A model of the rules on this page, not the system: one book (B7), one hold-shelf slot, two cards, and no expiry.

Place hold
A card may hold B7 once at a time. A new hold queues behind the others.
Shelve a returned copy
If the shelf slot is free, the oldest waiting hold becomes ready.
Pick up
The desk hands B7 over only for a ready hold on that card.
